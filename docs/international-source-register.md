# International lighting source register

Checked against publisher pages on 2026-11-09. This is a source-discovery register,
not an executable criteria pack or a choice of assignment design standard. No
numerical office thresholds have been adopted from search snippets or reseller
tables. Hong Kong adoption and contractual/project applicability are separate.

| Publication | Publisher evidence | Verified scope / remaining evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ISO/CIE 8995-1:2025 | [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/76342.html), [CIE](https://www.cie.co.at/publications/light-and-lighting-lighting-work-places-part-1-indoor-0) | Indoor workplace lighting; CIE states it replaces ISO 8995-1:2002. ISO excludes emergency lighting from this document. Exact office task rows and measurement definitions have not been verified here. |
| BS EN 12464-1:2021 | [BSI publisher record](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/light-and-lighting-lighting-of-work-places-indoor-work-places-2) | BSI describes replacement of the withdrawn 2011 edition and revised task/surround/background and glare guidance. No table values adopted. This is not automatically a Hong Kong statutory requirement. |
| SLL LG7 Lighting for offices (2023) | [CIBSE publisher record](https://www.cibse.org/knowledge-research/knowledge-portal/lg7-lighting-for-offices-2023) | CIBSE says recommended illumination levels reflect BS EN 12464-1:2021. Office task selection and full numerical/contextual guidance remain to be read before a profile is marked verified. |
| SLL Code for Lighting (2022) | [CIBSE publisher record](https://cibse.org/knowledge-research/knowledge-portal/sll-code-for-lighting-2022/) | Publisher identifies a 25 July 2023 corrigendum affecting chapters 3, 9 and 17. A future source copy must include/check that correction. |
| SLL LG14 Control of electric lighting (2023) | [CIBSE publisher record](https://www.cibse.org/knowledge-research/knowledge-portal/lg14-control-of-electric-lighting-2023/) | Relevant design guidance for controls. Does not replace the separately scoped Hong Kong BEC control requirements. No unreviewed provisions adopted. |

The [CIBSE publication list](https://www.cibse.org/get-involved/societies/society-of-light-and-lighting-sll/knowledge-resources/sll-publications-and-guidance/)
also lists these SLL editions and explains member access/purchase. Membership or
purchase access was not attempted. Old LG7 (2015) and ISO 8995-1:2002 figures must
not be labelled current merely because they appear frequently in web summaries.

Before creating an executable international profile, verify the licensed source
edition/corrigenda, exact task row, quantity definition, calculation surface,
maintenance basis, glare method and applicability. Preserve clause/page evidence.
The existing evaluator can represent sourced criteria, but a public catalogue
page alone does not substantiate numerical compliance thresholds.
